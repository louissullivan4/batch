  • White on accent = 7.77:1, on accent-pressed = 10.38:1, on success = 7.84:1, on destructive = 7.49:1 — filled buttons keep the 7:1 floor too.
  • Offline-neutral is a warm grey, deliberately outside the semantic hue families. It can never be mistaken for warning or error, and it always ships with the dashed-ring glyph and the word "Offline" — colour alone never encodes state.
  • Border (1.35:1) is decorative only; anything that must be findable in glare gets ink, not a hairline.
